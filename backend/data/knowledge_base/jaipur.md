# Jaipur Travel Intelligence & Heritage Guide

## Safety & Emergency Protocols
- **Emergency Police**: Dial 112 or 100. Rajasthan Tourist Assistance Police booths are stationed near Hawa Mahal and Amer Fort.
- **Medical Emergency**: Dial 108 for emergency ambulance. Major multi-specialty hospitals include SMS Hospital and Fortis Escorts Jaipur.
- **Street Vendor Scams**: Be cautious of unofficial "gemstone dealers" and unsolicited gemstone investment advice in the old bazaar corridors. Purchase certified gemstones only from Rajasthan Small Industries Corporation (Rajasthali) or verified outlets.

## Local Transit & Mobility Rules
- **Jaipur Metro Rail**: The Pink Line connects Mansarovar to Badi Chaupar (directly adjacent to Hawa Mahal and the walled city). Trains operate every 7-10 minutes; contactless smart tokens and QR tickets are available at station counters.
- **Auto-Rickshaws & E-Rickshaws**: E-rickshaws are the most efficient way to navigate the narrow alleys of Johari Bazaar and Tripolia Bazaar. Ensure drivers turn on meters or negotiate a fixed rate prior to boarding.
- **Amer Fort Transport**: To reach Amer Fort hilltop, visitors can take traditional government-regulated elephant rides (operating strictly until 11:30 AM), 4x4 open jeeps from the lower parking plaza, or walk up the cobblestone ramp.

## Visa, Entry & Monuments Composite Pass
- **Monument Composite Pass**: The Department of Archaeology and Museums issues a Composite Ticket valid for 2 days covering Amer Fort, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar, Albert Hall Museum, Nahargarh Fort, and Sisodia Rani Garden. Purchasing the composite pass at the first monument saves over 40% and avoids separate queues.
- **International Visitors**: Indian e-Tourist Visa required. Entry fees for historical monuments differ for domestic and international travelers (carry your passport or passport photocopy).

## Cultural Etiquette & Local Laws
- **Footwear at Sacred Sites**: Remove shoes before stepping onto temple marble courtyards (such as Govind Dev Ji Temple or Birla Mandir). Shoe stalls with safe tokens are provided.
- **Photography Permissions**: Tripods and professional video cameras require special archaeological permits at heritage sites; smartphone photography is permitted across all primary courtyards.
