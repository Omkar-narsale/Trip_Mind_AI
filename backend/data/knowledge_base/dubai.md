# Dubai Travel Intelligence & UAE Guide

## Safety & Emergency Protocols
- **Emergency Police**: Dial 999. Dubai is ranked among the safest metropolitan cities globally with low violent crime rates.
- **Ambulance**: Dial 998. Fire Department (Civil Defense): Dial 997. Tourist Security Department Helpline: 800-4888.
- **Heat & Dehydration Alert**: During summer months (May–September), outdoor temperatures exceed 42°C with high humidity. Schedule outdoor excursions for early morning (07:00–09:30 AM) or after sunset. Carry oral rehydration salts and sunscreen.

## Local Transit & Mobility Rules
- **Dubai Metro & Nol Cards**: The automated driverless Dubai Metro (Red and Green Lines) spans major business, shopping, and airport corridors. Riders must purchase a rechargeable Nol Card (Silver for regular class, Gold for VIP Gold Class carriages). Eating, drinking water, or chewing gum inside Metro trains or stations incurs a spot fine of AED 100.
- **Taxis & Careem/Uber**: Taxis operated by Dubai Taxi Corporation (DTC) are metered, clean, and accept international credit cards. Halal-friendly "Pink Taxis" driven by female drivers are available for women and families.

## Visa & Entry Regulations
- **Visa on Arrival**: Citizens of GCC countries, the European Union, the United States, the UK, Australia, and select others receive free 30-day or 90-day Visas on Arrival at Dubai International Airport (DXB). Indian passport holders with valid US, UK, or Schengen visas can obtain a 14-day Visa on Arrival.
- **Medication Restrictions**: The UAE Ministry of Health strictly controls certain prescription pharmaceuticals (such as codeine, tramadol, and psychotropic drugs). Always check the official UAE Ministry of Health restricted medication list and carry a physician's prescription.

## Cultural Etiquette & Local Laws
- **Public Decorum**: Dress modestly in public areas like shopping malls, traditional souks, and government buildings, ensuring shoulders and knees are covered. Beachwear is strictly confined to beach clubs and hotel pools.
- **Photography of Government Facilities**: Photographing military buildings, government ministries, airports, or individuals without their express consent is strictly prohibited by UAE law.
